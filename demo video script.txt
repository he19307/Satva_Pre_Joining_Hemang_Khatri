"Welcome to the project dashboard.

This is the Invoice Processing Page, where invoices can be processed, 
and data is extracted. We have integrated Tesseract OCR for text extraction 
from invoices and developed an AI-powered OCR model to enhance accuracy and readability. 
The extracted data is then mapped to an Invoice Card for structured representation.

Next, we have the Expense Categorization Page, where expenses are classified using an AI model.
For this task, I have implemented BERT, ensuring precise and context-aware categorization.

On the Automatic Reconciliation Page, transactions are reconciled intelligently.
The system identifies and displays both matched and unmatched records for review.

We also have an AI-powered Chatbot that provides instant answers to accounting-related queries.

All extracted, categorized, and reconciled data is consolidated and displayed on the Dashboard 
Page for a comprehensive overview of financial activities."
